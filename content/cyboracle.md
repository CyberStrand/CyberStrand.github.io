<p>As a Research Assistant at Dartmouth's DREAM Studio, I worked on <a href="https://hop.dartmouth.edu/events/cyboracle-nonaverse" target="_blank"><strong>Cyboracle in the Nonaverse</strong></a>, an immersive VR and AI concert experience built around Cyboracle — the digital avatar of musician Nona Hendryx. </p>

<h2>My Role</h2>

<p>I was responsible for <strong>setting up the motion capture system</strong> for the shoot — calibrating the camera volume and getting clean marker tracking on a live performer — and for <strong>post-processing the captured animation in Maya</strong> afterward, cleaning up the raw mocap solve and retargeting it onto Cyboracle's rig for the final VR performance.</p>
