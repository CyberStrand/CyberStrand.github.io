<h2>Recreating a Ghibli Interior</h2>

<p>For a 3D Modeling course, I rebuilt the living room from Studio Ghibli's <em>Howl's Moving Castle</em> entirely by hand in Maya — every piece of furniture, wall panel, and prop in the scene was modeled from scratch, with no pre-made assets.</p>

<p>The goal was to capture the room's warm, lived-in atmosphere: mosaic-tiled walls, a winding staircase, a candlelit dining table set with fruit and wine, and Calcifer's hearth glowing at the center of it all.</p>
